# Portfolio / provenance notice

This repository is a historical engineering portfolio, not a current production design library.

- Mars Rover Manipal material comes from a multidisciplinary student-team project. Project notes distinguish personal electronics contributions from complete-team hardware.
- Some historical CAD projects used imported or third-party component symbols/footprints. Questionable redistributable libraries were deliberately omitted from the public-ready package.
- Professional/confidential employer material is not included.
- Historical reports are retained as evidence of work and learning at the time; they are not presented as current design standards.
- Where an old revision or project association could not be established confidently, that uncertainty is stated rather than reconstructed as fact.
